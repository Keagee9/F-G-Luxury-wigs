# **App Name**: Luxe Locks Boutique

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Showcase a wide variety of human hair wigs with detailed descriptions, high-quality images, and customer reviews.
- Virtual Try-On: AI-powered virtual try-on feature that allows customers to upload their photo and see how different wigs look on them.
- Personalized Recommendations: Offer wig recommendations based on customer preferences, face shape, skin tone, and style using an AI tool.
- Secure Checkout: A safe and secure payment gateway to ensure customers can purchase with confidence.
- Appointment Booking: Allow customers to book virtual consultations with hair stylists for personalized advice.
- Customer Accounts: Enable customers to create accounts, save their favorite wigs, track orders, and manage their profile.
- Style Guide and Blog: Provide styling tips, maintenance advice, and articles to help customers care for their wigs.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep burgundy (#800020) to evoke luxury and sophistication.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC) for a clean, elegant backdrop.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) used sparingly for highlights and calls to action.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' serif for a fashionable, high-end feel.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' sans-serif to complement headline font.
- Use elegant, minimalist icons related to wig types, care products, and style guides.
- Employ a clean, spacious layout with high-quality product photography to showcase the wigs.
- Subtle transitions and animations on product pages and during the virtual try-on process for a smooth, luxurious experience.
